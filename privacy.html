This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to the information you send us. It is provided for transparency and is general information, not legal advice.

Who we are

Grand Nash Studio ("we," "us") is a web design studio based in Houston, Texas. You can reach us at hello@grandnashstudio.com or (832) 910-8301.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us through our inquiry form: your name, your business name, your email address, your phone number (if you provide it), and the details of your request. We ask you not to include sensitive information such as passwords, payment card numbers, or government ID numbers, and we do not request them.

Why we collect it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your request, build the demo or quote you asked for, and communicate with you about our services. We do not use it for anything you did not reach out for.

How your information is handled and shared

Your form submission is delivered to our email inbox through a third-party form service (FormSubmit) that processes the message on our behalf. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We share it only where necessary to respond to you, or if required by law.

Agreeing to be contacted

By submitting the form, you agree that we may contact you about your request by email, phone, or text message using the details you provide. This consent is limited to your inquiry with us. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying to any message or emailing us, and we will honor it.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your inquiry only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our correspondence. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@grandnashstudio.com, and we will remove it unless we are required to keep it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, which is another reason we ask you not to send sensitive details through the form.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add basic analytics in the future to understand site traffic, we will update this policy first.

Children

This site and our services are intended for business owners and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of certain uses. To make any such request, email us at hello@grandnashstudio.com and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
